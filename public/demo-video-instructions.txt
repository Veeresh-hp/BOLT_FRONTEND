DEMO VIDEO SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
===============================

To add your hand gesture demo video:

1. Place your demo video file in this folder (public/) with one of these names:
   - demo-video.mp4 (preferred)
   - demo-video.webm (alternative)

2. The video should demonstrate:
   - Proper hand positioning in front of camera
   - Clear execution of supported gestures:
     * Open Palm (Stop/Wait)
     * Thumbs Up (Good/Yes/Approve)
     * Peace Sign (Victory/Two)
     * Pointing (Attention/Direction)
     * Fist (Power/Strength)
     * OK Sign (Okay/Perfect)
     * Rock On (Cool/Awesome)
     * Number 1, 2, 3

3. Recommended video specifications:
   - Duration: 30-60 seconds
   - Resolution: 720p or 1080p
   - Format: MP4 (H.264 encoding)
   - Size: Under 10MB for web optimization

4. The video will:
   - Open in a professional modal overlay
   - Include playback controls
   - Show instructions below the video
   - Have a close button for easy dismissal

Current file structure should be:
public/
├── demo-video.mp4 (your video file)
├── favicon.ico
├── index.html
├── logo192.png
├── logo512.png
├── manifest.json
└── robots.txt

Once you add the video file, the demo button will show your actual demo video instead of the placeholder message.
